freedom

August 20, 2025

during covid i built out a ford transit. not a car with a mattress in the back, but a home and office on wheels. kitchen, sink, workspace with internet, a small bathroom. i strapped a mountain bike and a stand-up paddleboard to the outside, packed backpacking and hiking gear inside. everything i need to live, work, and play without booking a hotel or renting a car.

i fly out to wherever i left it, parked in someone's driveway for a monthly fee. i grab my dog and drive. a week on trails, lakes, mountains. then i drop it back in the driveway and fly home. the flight is the cost. no hotels, no rentals, no restaurants.

every day i get a new backyard. paddleboard on a lake one morning, mountain bike through the woods the next, or hike up a ridge with my pack. manhattan gives me the density. the van gives me the opposite.
