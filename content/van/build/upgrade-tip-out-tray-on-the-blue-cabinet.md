- **What:** Add a **tip-out tray** to the blue kitchen cabinet front (behind the front he pointed at. see photo): the front panel **pivots out ~45°** on tip-out hinges to reveal a **shallow tray** behind it for small items (keys, chapstick, sponge, pens, small tools).
- **Hardware:** a **tip-out tray kit**. pair of **tip-out tray hinges** + a **tray** (plastic or stainless) sized/trimmed to the front width. Off-the-shelf: **Rev-A-Shelf**, Hafele, or a generic Amazon kit (trays come ~11" / 14" / trimmable).
- **⚠️ CLEARANCE. the real question:** a tip-out tray's tray sits **behind the front and swings into the cabinet**, so it needs a **few inches of clear depth right behind the panel**. In the photo the **grey bin is pressed right up to the front** → a standard tip-out tray may **not have room to fit or swing**. Confirm there's clear depth behind the chosen front, or the bin needs to sit back / a shallower tray.
- **Which front:** confirm **which panel** becomes the tip-out. a **false/fixed front** is the normal spot (a real door can also be converted, but then it stops functioning as a door).
- **Need to buy:** tip-out tray kit (hinges + tray, e.g. Rev-A-Shelf), sized to the front width; screws
- **Tools:** drill ✅ (hinge mounting)
- **Measure/decide:** **front-panel width** → tray size (trimmable); **clear depth behind the panel** (does a tray fit + swing without hitting the grey bin?); which front (false-front vs door)
- **❓ Confirm:** which front panel, and is there clear depth behind it (or does the bin move back)?
