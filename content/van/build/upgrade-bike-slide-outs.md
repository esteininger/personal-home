- **What:** Install the heavy-duty slide-out I already have but never mounted.
- **⚠️ Anchoring (don't rely on ¾" birch + screws):** a bike on an extended slide is a big cantilevered load and road vibration loosens screws.
  - **Through-bolt to the 8020 floor rail** (use my T-nuts) and/or the **factory floor tie-down points** where the slide's holes line up.
  - Where landing on bare ¾" birch subfloor, use **through-bolts + fender washers / backing plate** underneath. not screws into the top face.
  - Consider a **plywood mounting platform** that ties into both the 8020 and the subfloor to spread the load.
- **Have:** heavy-duty slide-out ✅, T-nuts ✅
- **Need to buy:** through-bolts + fender washers / backing plate, maybe plywood for a platform
- **Tools:** drill ✅
- **Check/measure:** slide weight rating + hole pattern; **closed length + full extension** (clearance front/back); bike wheelbase → platform size; does it land on the 8020 rail or just ¾" birch?
