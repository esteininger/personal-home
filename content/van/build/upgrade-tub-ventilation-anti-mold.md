- **What:** The storage tubs in the "garage" trap moisture and grow mold. Add ventilation so they can breathe.
- **Plan:**
  - **Drill a grid of vent holes in the lids** (and high on the sides) for cross-flow. a matrix of ~½"-¾" holes.
  - **Cover the holes** with mesh/screen or stick-on louver vents so dust/critters stay out while air moves.
  - **Attack the moisture too:** add a moisture absorber (DampRid / silica gel), store items DRY, don't seal lids airtight.
  - *(Alternative for the worst spots: swap solid tubs for ventilated/perforated crates.)*
- **Need to buy:** hole saw / spade or step bits, mesh/screen or louver vents, moisture absorbers (DampRid/silica), maybe a couple ventilated crates
- **Tools:** drill ✅, hole saw / spade bits
- **Check/measure:** # of tubs + lid material (confirm drillable plastic); hole size + spacing; which tubs are worst for mold
