- **What:** Move the MAXTRAX boards from the interior mount up to the **roof**. Plan the roof layout **together with P4 (Starlink)**. both share the roof.
- **Base:** 8020 crossbars on the roof (same profile as the build). or clamp to an existing roof rack / factory rails. **Avoid new roof penetrations where possible** (clamp to rails/track); any bolt through the roof gets **butyl + Sikaflex** sealed (same as the Starlink mount).
- **Board attach (reuse the P13 anti-bounce design, now on the roof):** studs (SS threaded rod *or* **MAXTRAX mounting pins**) through the boards' molded holes into the crossbars, with **standoff spacers** (kill bounce) + **locking pins** (theft. roof is exposed) + anti-rattle washers.
- **Roof-specific watch-outs:** wind lift (must be tight, no flap); **theft** (locking pins); added **height/clearance** (note garage + overhead clearance with boards up top); weight up high; **access** to load/unload.
- **Have:** MAXTRAX boards ✅, T-nuts ✅
- **Need to buy:** roof 8020 crossbars + feet/clamps (if no roof rack); MAXTRAX roof mounting/locking pins *or* SS rod + standoff spacers + knobs; roof sealant (butyl/Sikaflex) if penetrating; hardware
- **Tools:** drill ✅, hacksaw/cutoff for rod
- **Measure/decide:** roof space L×W for the boards **+ coordinate footprint with Starlink (P4)** so both fit; existing roof rack/8020 profile or add crossbars; board stack dims (hole spacing + stack height, from P13); **overhead/garage clearance with boards on top**; clamp-to-rails vs penetrate
