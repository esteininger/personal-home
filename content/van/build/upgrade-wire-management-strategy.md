- **What:** Clean up the 12V accessory wiring + the loose cable runs (see photos. daisy-chained feeds behind the socket panel, cables draped across the wall).
- **Plan:**
  - **One hub:** replace daisy-chained socket feeds with a **blade fuse block + ground bus** (6-12 circuit). each socket/USB on its own fused, labeled circuit.
  - **Route on edges:** secure runs with **adhesive cable-tie mounts / P-clips** along cabinet edges + corners; keep split-loom on exposed spans (already have some). Nothing draped across open wall.
  - **Separate power from data:** 12V runs on one side, Starlink cable / coax on the other; leave a **service loop** at the roof entry.
  - **Label both ends** of every cable.
- **Need to buy:** blade fuse block + ground bus (size to circuit count), assorted blade fuses, adhesive cable-tie mounts / P-clips, split-loom, ferrules/heat-shrink crimps, cable labels
- **Tools:** drill ✅, crimpers (confirm have)
- **Check/measure:** count accessory circuits (sizes the fuse block); measure run lengths for loom + wire; confirm I have crimpers/heat-shrink
