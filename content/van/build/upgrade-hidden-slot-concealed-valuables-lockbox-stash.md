- **What:** Use the hidden slot behind the lift-up felt flap under the seat as a **concealed valuables stash**. passport, cash, documents, spare key, small electronics. The flap already hides it (stays stock-looking); the **pre-run 12V** becomes a **motion LED** so the dark cavity lights up when you open it. Rough size **~7.6" W × ~2.5" H × ~15-31" L**. *confirm exact inches*.
- **Primary build. locked + anchored box:** drop a **slim steel lockbox / vehicle console safe** into the slot and **bolt it to solid structure** (backing plate/fender washers) so it can't be lifted out even if found; keyed or combo. Retain the felt flap over it (velcro) so it still looks stock. *(Lower-effort version: a soft zip organizer/pouch. hidden but not locked.)*
- **12V use. see-inside light:** wire the pre-run 12V to a small **motion-activated LED strip/puck** inside (lights on flap-open). Tiny draw; inline-fuse it. *(Or leave the 12V capped for a future use.)*
- **Alt uses (if you change your mind):** foam-lined **12V charging drawer** (fused USB-C/USB + 12V hub); or a **12V mini-fan / dehumidifier rod** to keep the cavity dry (P15 mold concern).
- **⚠️ Safety / cautions:** the **fire-extinguisher pull-pin + release sit right below**. keep them clear. **Before drilling to anchor the box, check what's behind the panel** (avoid fuel/brake lines, wiring harness, tank). **Fuse the 12V at the source**; confirm **switched vs. always-on**; grommet/strain-relief the cable. Wiring ties into P8.
- **Need to buy:** slim steel **lockbox / console safe** sized to the slot (keyed or combo) + **security cable/tether**; mounting bolts + backing plate/fender washers; **12V motion LED** strip/puck + inline blade fuse holder + fuse + grommet; velcro to retain the felt flap. *(Soft option: a zip organizer pouch instead of the box.)*
- **Tools:** drill ✅, crimpers/heat-shrink
- **Measure/decide:** exact slot **W × H × L** + flap opening → **max lockbox footprint** that fits & clears the flap; a **solid anchor point** behind the panel (and what to avoid drilling into); where the 12V cable emerges + slack; **switched or always-on (measure voltage)**; extinguisher-pin clearance
