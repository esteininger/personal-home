- **What:** Cushion cut to fit + waterproof + more comfortable, add a fold-up/extending backrest, and paint the box.
- **Cushion:** high-density foam cut to the bench-top footprint (currently overhangs); wrap in **marine vinyl** (wipeable, waterproof. best for a wet/bathroom area) or a removable waterproof zippered cover.
- **Backrest (converts):** piano-hinge a plywood backrest to the back edge; hold it upright with **folding shelf brackets / a locking friction hinge** (the "lever") that releases to fold flat and extend the seat.
- **Paint:** prime (birch is porous) + enamel; **seal/edge-band the raw plywood edges first** (see photo. currently bare). Same blue as kitchen if desired.
- **Secure to van (bench shifts while driving):** there's an **8020 rail right behind the bench**. bolt the bench to it with **2 angle brackets + drop-in T-nuts**. Use **star-knob bolts** into the T-nuts if you want it hand-removable. A strap alone won't stop it creeping; hard-mount is the fix. If there's a gap to the rail, add a spacer block. *(see P6-bench-securing photo)*
- **Need to buy:** high-density foam (cut to size), marine vinyl / waterproof cover, piano hinge, folding/locking brackets, primer + enamel, edge banding; **2 angle brackets + T-nuts + star-knob bolts** (secure to 8020)
- **Tools:** oscillating saw ✅, drill ✅, electric carving knife (foam. same one as the mattress)
- **Measure:** bench top L × W (cushion), desired cushion thickness, backrest H × W + how far the seat extends, box surfaces to paint; **gap bench↔8020 + 8020 profile** (for the anchor brackets)
