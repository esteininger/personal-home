- **What:** Improve odor containment. Two separate seal points. spec'd both:
  - **Toilet valve:** the Camco blade/slide valve between bowl and tank dries out → odor. Fix = seal lubricant/conditioner or a replacement blade-seal kit (**need the exact Camco model #**).
  - **Cabinet lid:** foam weatherstrip already on the box lip. upgrade to **closed-cell foam gasket + a compression/draw latch** to pull the lid down tight.
- **Need to buy:** Camco toilet seal lube / blade-seal kit (per model #), closed-cell foam gasket tape, 1-2 compression/draw latches
- **Tools:** drill ✅
- **Measure:** lid opening perimeter (gasket length), current gap size; **note the Camco model # / capacity** off the label
