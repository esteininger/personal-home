- **What:** One color-matched blue across the whole build. Buy **one batch** of enamel + primer, prep + paint everything in a single session.
- **Surfaces getting the blue:**
  - Kitchen cabinet faces (existing blue. the match target)
  - **P1**. backsplash / splash-back panel
  - **P6**. bathroom bench box
  - **Between-seats birch console/box** (raw, water-stained. see photo)
  - **Cab birch shelf/panel** (bracket-mounted up front. raw birch; removable)
- **Best method. paint off the van:** the console, cab shelf, and any bracket-mounted panels **unbolt** → prime/paint/dry them flat off the van (no tape-off, no overspray in the cab), then reinstall.
- **Prep (all birch):** prime first (porous), **seal/edge-band raw edges**, light sand between coats; wipeable **satin/semi-gloss enamel**.
- **Match:** find the original blue's code, or bring a cabinet door / chip to color-match.
- **Measure:** total surface area of all surfaces so I buy enough enamel + primer.
