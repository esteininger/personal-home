12V DC

All combinations need to be in parallel (same voltage, varying current).

120v AC:

- 2x 120V AC adapters (1 by driver seat at end of kitchen and 1 by bed opposite side)
- 1x in garage

Starter Battery:

- Grounding to driver seat
- Positive to driver seat

use d-ring and [anti seize](https://www.amazon.com/Permatex-80078-Anti-Seize-Lubricant-Bottle/dp/B000FW7VGE) dielectric grease

Best practice is to clean paint off metal and through bolt lug with top lock nut & washer. Then spray with clear acrylic to reduce chance of corrosion.

Victron DC-DC Battery Charger:

- Requires a 6 AWG (maybe use a "power post" to step down from 4 AWG to 6
