I used 4 layers of flooring as follows: 

1. .40” Minicell which acts as a sound deadening and insulation to fill the corrugations 
2. .25” Minicell similar as above but flat
3. 1/2” Baltic birch to server as a subfloor 
4. Vinyl sheet for moisture prevention 

The reason why we don’t use thinsulate is because most insulation like thinsulate requires full expansion in order to be effective, if it’s in the floor it both won’t expand and won’t “breathe”.

All edges are filled with spray insulation and sealed with silicone.
