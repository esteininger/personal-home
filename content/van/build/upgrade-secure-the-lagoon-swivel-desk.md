- **What:** The Lagoon swivel table wobbles/shifts on bumpy roads. **Plan: strap it down for driving** (chosen over a folding leg).
- **Do:**
  - **Check the swivel lock** first. the Lagoon pivot loosens; fully tighten it (may cut most of the wobble on its own).
  - **Cam-strap it down** to the seat frame / base for driving. no rebound (more secure than a bungee). You already have a strap on that seat; a proper cam strap holds better.
  - Easiest for driving: **swivel/fold it flat against the seatback, then cam-strap** so it can't move at all.
- **Need to buy:** cam strap(s); *(optional)* friction washer for the swivel pivot
- **Tools:** none required (drill only if adding a fixed strap anchor)
- **Check/measure:** swivel-lock condition; a good strap anchor point on the seat base / frame
