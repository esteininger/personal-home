- **What:** Overhead cabinets hung off 8020. There's already an 8020 rail on one wall. **add a matching rail on the other side**, then hang light birch boxes off both rails.
- **Keep them light up high:** boxes from **½" (or ¼") Baltic birch, not ¾"** (weight overhead hurts handling + is worse in a hard stop). **Positive latches** so doors can't fly open while driving.
- **Have:** T-nuts ✅ (one 8020 rail already mounted)
- **Need to buy:** 8020 extrusion for the 2nd side (**match existing profile**), corner brackets/gussets, latches, ½"/¼" birch for the boxes
- **Tools:** drill ✅, oscillating saw ✅
- **Check/measure:** length for the 2nd 8020 rail (match existing run); existing rail profile (10- vs 15-series); gap between the two rails; headroom → cabinet depth/height; confirm enough T-nuts + brackets
