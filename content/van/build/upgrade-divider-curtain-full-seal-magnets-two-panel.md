- **What:** Add magnets to the **interior cabin divider curtain** (two grommet panels on a cross-van rod. *distinct from the P20 cab divider*; see photo) so it **completely seals/closes** with no light or privacy gaps. Three closure points to seal: the **middle** (where the two panels meet), the **sides**, and the **ends** (which snap to the van's steel body). *(Van body is steel. the end magnets grab bare metal directly.)*
- **Middle (center seam):** sew/glue mating **neodymium magnets** down the leading vertical edge of **both** panels so they snap together and close the center gap. Check polarity so the mating magnets attract (not repel). Space ~every 8-12".
- **Sides (panel edge → wall):** magnets along the outer vertical edges to pull each panel flat against the adjacent wall. Where that surface is **bare steel** → magnet grabs directly; where it's **paneled birch / carpet** → mount a small **steel catch plate/disc** (VHB tape or screwed) as the target.
- **Ends (→ van metal):** the end/edge magnets snap to the **bare steel** of the van body (rib / door frame / pillar), pinning the curtain shut and killing the edge light gap.
- **Magnet spec:** disc/block **neodymium** (housed/countersunk) or **sew-in magnetic snaps**. thin magnetic tape is too weak for a heavy blackout curtain. House/countersink them so they don't tear the fabric; verify mating polarity attracts.
- **Need to buy:** neodymium magnets (housed/countersunk or sew-in magnetic snaps); **steel catch plates/washers + VHB tape or screws** (for any non-steel side/wall surfaces); fabric tabs/pockets + thread or fabric glue to mount them
- **Tools:** needle/thread or fabric adhesive; drill ✅ (only if screwing steel catches)
- **Measure/decide:** panel leading-edge + side-edge heights → **# of magnets** (~every 10"); which closing surfaces are **bare steel vs paneled** (need a catch); center-seam overlap; **light-gap widths** at middle / sides / ends
