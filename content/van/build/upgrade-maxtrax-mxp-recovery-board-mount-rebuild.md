- **⚠️ Superseded by P18 (moving the boards to the roof).** Keep as a **fallback** if the roof mount doesn't pan out; the anti-bounce design below is reused by P18.
- **What:** Current 80/20 mount for the MAXTRAX boards isn't stable. it bounces/rattles. Rebuild it stiff, with easy tool-free lock/unlock. *(A board working loose while driving is a safety issue. worth doing right.)*
- **Design (standoff-stud):**
  - **Studs:** stainless threaded rod (or MAXTRAX pins) anchored into the 80/20 via **stud T-nuts / drop-in T-nuts**, projecting through the boards' molded holes.
  - **Spacers (the anti-bounce part):** aluminum or stainless **standoff spacers** sized to boss depth + stack height. they take the clamp load so the board is captured solid, not crushed or loose.
  - **Lock/unlock:** star-knob / hand knobs (tool-free) OR a **quick-release ball-detent pin** through a cross-drilled stud. Consider MAXTRAX **locking pins** (theft. boards are exposed).
  - **Anti-rattle:** rubber/neoprene washer between board + spacer.
- **Two paths:** fully custom (SS rod + spacers + knobs = sturdier/cheaper) OR MAXTRAX official **mounting pins + locking pins** (faster, off-the-shelf).
- **Have:** MAXTRAX boards ✅, existing 80/20 mount ✅
- **Need to buy:** stainless threaded rod (cut to length) *or* MAXTRAX mounting/locking pins; aluminum/SS standoff spacers; star knobs *or* quick-release/locking pins; stud/drop-in T-nuts (match profile); rubber/neoprene anti-rattle washers
- **Tools:** drill ✅, hacksaw/cutoff for rod
- **Measure:** 80/20 profile; board **hole spacing** (c-to-c) + hole dia; **# boards stacked** + board/boss thickness → stack height → rod + spacer length; standoff distance + clearance behind
