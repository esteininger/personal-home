- **What:** A single divider panel between the bike bay and the electrical bay. **Safety driver:** a bike tire currently rests against exposed terminals/wiring (short + abrasion risk). A divider = the fix; full enclosure not needed.
- **Material:** ¼" **polycarbonate (Lexan)**. clear (still see the LEDs/gauges), impact-resistant (won't crack under the bike), non-conductive. NOT acrylic/plexiglass (shatters under point load). Opaque alt: ¼" Baltic birch (cheaper, stiffer).
- **Mounting rules:**
  - Slide into the 8020 T-slot on **two opposite edges** (top + bottom) so a tire can't flex it inward. One free edge = it bows into the electronics.
  - If only one rail is there, bolt a short piece of 8020 / an angle bracket to create the 2nd capture edge.
  - Notch one edge for cable pass-through + airflow; deburr sharp edges.
- **Also:** strap the bike wheel to a rail so it isn't resting on the divider (divider = backup, not primary restraint). Insulating boots on all positive terminals/busbars regardless.
- **Need to buy:** ¼" polycarbonate panel (cut to size), T-nuts/hardware, maybe a short 8020 length + bracket for the 2nd rail, terminal boots/busbar covers, a cam/bungee strap for the wheel
- **Tools:** oscillating saw ✅, drill ✅
- **Confirm at drop-off:** 8020 profile (10-series slot ≈ 0.256" fits ¼"; 15-series is wider → shim or use panel clips)
