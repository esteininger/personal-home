This town is trash, no offense to anyone that lives there but the only appeal is proximity to the west yellowstown entrance. Trash everywhere, loud noises, plenty of delapidated buildings, WiFi/coffee shops were sparse. 

The streets were empty at night and the only time we've ever been asked to move overnight has been here. Not van friendly at all. 

We went to the grizzly discovery center which was cool but all the animals were sleeping.
