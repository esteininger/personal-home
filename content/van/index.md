I converted a Ford Transit into a home and office on wheels during COVID. Kitchen, a bed, a desk with internet, 400 watts of solar on the roof. I fly out to wherever I parked it, grab my dog, and work from trailheads for a week at a time.

This is the build documented step by step, the places it has taken me, and the gear and notes from the road.
