[My name is Ethan, and I'm a software developer](https://ethan.dev) and my life goal is to experience as much of this planet as possible, with the current method being on wheels.

In Spring 2021 I decided to sell all my belongings and purchase a van with the intention of converting it into a mobile home.

This van is optimized for my passions, which include: *hiking*, *biking*, *paddling* and *coding*.

This page documents my conversion, costs, adventures, and lessons. I hope it inspires others to pursue their own [BHAGs](https://esteininger.medium.com/why-pursuing-a-bhag-is-worth-the-struggle-922401444f82)!
