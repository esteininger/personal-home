**Mountain Biking**

---

Waldo Lake perimeter, see on [TrailForks](https://www.trailforks.com/region/waldo-lake/?activitytype=1&z=11.2&lat=43.72779&lon=-122.02245).

Woke up early while it was in the low 30's and biked the entire 20 mi perimeter of the lake:

![](https://diyjmz7hrjx3w.cloudfront.net/van/nature/three-sisters/19.webp)
