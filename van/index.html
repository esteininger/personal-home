Software Developer in a Van

My name is Ethan, and I'm a software developer and my life goal is to experience as much of this planet as possible, with the current method being on wheels.

In Spring 2021 I decided to sell all my belongings and purchase a van with the intention of converting it into a mobile home.

This van is optimized for my passions, which include: hiking, biking, paddling and coding.

This page documents my conversion, costs, adventures, and lessons. I hope it inspires others to pursue their own BHAGs!
