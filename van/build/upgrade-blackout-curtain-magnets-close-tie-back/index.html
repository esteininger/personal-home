Blackout curtain magnets (close + tie-back)

StatusPlanned
CategoryCab
  • What: Add magnets to the cab-divider curtain so it (a) closes/seals against the van body with no light gaps, and (b) holds open, bunched against the wall with a magnetic tie-back. (Van body is steel. magnets grab bare metal directly.)
  • Close (seal the edges): sew or glue strong neodymium magnets into the curtain's leading vertical edge (in fabric tabs or behind the hem) so they snap to the steel B-pillar / door frame, closing the light gap. Space ~every 8-12".
  • Tie-back (hold to wall): gather the open curtain and hold it with a magnetic tie-back. a magnet-tipped strap that clamps the bunch to a steel point, or a pair of magnets sandwiching the fabric onto a wall catch. ⚠️ Where the wall is paneled birch/carpet (non-steel), mount a small steel plate/disc (VHB or screwed) as the magnet catch.
  • Note: thin magnetic tape is too weak for a heavy thermal curtain. use disc/block neodymium or sew-in magnetic snaps; check polarity so mating magnets attract.
  • Need to buy: neodymium magnets (housed/countersunk or sew-in magnetic snaps), magnetic tie-backs, steel catch plates/washers + VHB tape or screws (for paneled walls), webbing/fabric tabs + thread or fabric glue
  • Tools: drill ✅ (for wall catches), needle/thread or fabric adhesive
  • Measure/decide: curtain leading-edge height → # of magnets (~every 10"); is the closing surface bare steel (B-pillar/door)?; are the tie-back wall points steel or paneled (need a catch)?; light-gap width to close
