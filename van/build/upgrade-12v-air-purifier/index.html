12V air purifier

StatusPlanned
CategoryElectrical
  • What: Add a 12V (or USB) air purifier. Targets: wildfire smoke (big out West), cooking smoke, dog dander/odor, road dust, pollen.
  • Hard requirement: True HEPA + activated carbon (HEPA = smoke particulate + dander; carbon = smoke/cooking odor). Skip ionizer-only "car purifiers". they don't handle smoke.
  • Approach: DIY (chosen). 12V fan pulling through a HEPA+carbon filter in a small box; cheap, near-silent, low draw, replaceable filters, mounts to 8020.
  • ⚠️ Fan: the small caged 12V fan on hand is low static pressure. it'll move air but airflow drops a lot through a dense filter. OK to start with; a PC "muffin" fan or small blower pushes through filter resistance much better (better smoke performance).
  • Idea: buy a 2nd caged fan + attach HEPA. works IF sealed. Loosely strapping a filter to a caged fan = air bypasses around the edges (low static pressure) and barely filters. Two ways to do it right:
  • Best. mini "Corsi-Rosenthal" box: fan on top of a small box whose walls are HEPA+carbon filter panels. Big filter area = low resistance, so the weak fan pulls plenty through it. Seal all seams (tape/foam). Simple to build, matches the style.
  • Simpler. sealed shroud: a birch/cardboard duct from the fan intake to an oversized filter, foam-weatherstrip the edges (zero bypass), filter on the intake side (pull through).
  • Regardless: oversize the filter area (less back-pressure) and use HEPA + carbon. Two fans ≈ 2× airflow.
  • Placement (see P17-air-purifier-placement photo):
  • BEST. mount HIGH on the upper wall 8020 / cleat (by the overhead-cabinet line): smoke + warm air rise, best mixing, keeps floor/walkway clear.
  • Easy alt. bed-frame 8020 at the foot/side of the bed: near power, blows into the cabin, but lower = less ideal for smoke.
  • Either way: intake + exhaust clear of bedding/gear; secured so it doesn't rattle.
  • Power: wire to a switched, fused circuit off the P8 fuse block (do it with the wire cleanup).
  • Need to buy: HEPA + activated-carbon filter (sized to the box), a PC/blower 12V fan if the caged fan is too weak, filter-box material (birch/acrylic offcuts), inline switch + wire (via P8 kit), spare filters
  • Have: small caged 12V fan (starter. may upgrade)
  • Tools: drill ✅, oscillating saw ✅ (box)
  • Decide/measure: pick HIGH vs bed-frame mount; box size (to the filter); confirm the caged fan's airflow through the filter is enough or grab a PC/blower fan
  • Open: want me to spec a concrete DIY parts list (fan model + filter size + box dims) once you pick the spot?
